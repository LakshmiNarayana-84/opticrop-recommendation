How to measure soil and climate inputs

Nitrogen (N)

Use a reliable soil test kit or laboratory analysis. Combine multiple soil cores for a representative sample.

  • Units: mg/kg
  • Ideal workflow: collect, dry, mix, and test.

Phosphorus (P)

Measure with an Olsen or Bray extraction kit for accuracy. Home kits are acceptable for estimation.

  • Units: mg/kg
  • Best practice: sample multiple locations across the field.

Potassium (K)

Use a laboratory or field kit to estimate potassium levels. Ensure a representative soil sample is used.

  • Units: mg/kg
  • Sampling: combine several cores from the field.

Humidity

Use a hygrometer for air humidity or a soil moisture probe for soil conditions.

  • Units: % relative humidity
  • Use current hourly data for best results.

Soil pH

Measure soil pH with a digital meter or calibrated strip for the most dependable readings.

  • Scale: 0–14
  • Recommended soil range: 5.5–7.5 for most crops.

Rainfall

Record rainfall in millimeters using a rain gauge or local weather station.

  • Units: mm
  • Use recent precipitation totals for accurate recommendations.
